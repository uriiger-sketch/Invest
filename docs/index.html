Each carries a literature prior for its information coefficient (IC) per horizon, updated by the IC measured on this system's own history (precision-weighted Bayes). Weights are Σ⁻¹·IC, so correlated signals share weight instead of double-counting. A LightGBM ranker joins only in proportion to its purged out-of-sample IC. The grade combines the four horizon scores weighted by their estimated skill and correlation; α = IC·σ·z (Grinold) and P(beat) = Φ(IC·z) follow from the same model. Missing data counts as neutral, never as a fabricated value; the confidence column shows how much of the model each grade actually rests on.
